Game Rules

Welcome to The Word Duel! The goal is to outlast your opponent by forming valid words.

How to Play

The program will start with a word. Your task is to respond with a new, unique word that starts with the required prefix. The game alternates between you and the program until a player cannot find a valid word or the time runs out.

Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: Your word must start with the last letter of the previous word. You have a longer time limit.
  • Hard: Your word must start with the last two letters of the previous word. The time limit is shorter.

Scoring & Time

  • You earn points equal to the length of your word.
  • If you respond quickly, you get a time bonus.
  • Incorrect words or redundant words will result in a time penalty.
  • The game ends when the time runs out, a player can't find a word, or you choose to start a new game. The highest score wins!

Hints

  • You get two hints per game.
  • Click the "Get Hint" button to reveal a possible word.
